Features
⏱

Focus Sprint

Fullscreen timer that hides all controls. Move your cursor to the top edge to reveal them.

🔥

Streak Tracker

Counts consecutive days of planning. Miss a day and the streak resets — stay consistent.

🔊

Ambient Audio

Built-in Brown Noise, Rain, and 432Hz tone. No Spotify or browser tab needed.

Keyboard First

Navigate tasks with J/K, complete with Space, open everything via Ctrl+K command palette.

💾

Backup & Restore

Export your tasks as a .json file. Import them back anytime, even on a new machine.

Fully Offline

No account. No cloud. No telemetry. Your data stays on your machine, always.
