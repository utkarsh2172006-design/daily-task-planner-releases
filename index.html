Features
⏱

Focus Sprint Timer

A fullscreen, distraction-free countdown. Controls auto-hide when the timer starts and reappear when you move your cursor to the top edge.

🔥

Streak Tracker

Tracks consecutive days of planning. Miss a day and it resets — a powerful daily accountability system built directly into your workflow.

🔊

Ambient Audio Engine

Built-in Brown Noise, Rain, and 432Hz tone synthesized on-device. No Spotify, no browser tabs, no distractions.

⌨️

Keyboard-First Design

Navigate with J/K, complete with Space, edit with E, delete with Del. Ctrl+K opens a command palette for everything else.

💾

Backup & Restore

Export your tasks to a .json file in one click. Import them back anytime — even on a new machine.

🗂

Priority System

Tag every task as High, Medium, or Low priority. Visual color coding makes your most important work impossible to ignore.
