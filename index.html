Features
⏱

Focus Sprint

A fullscreen timer that clears the screen the moment it starts. Controls auto-hide. Move your cursor to the top edge to access them.

🔥

Streak Tracker

Tracks consecutive days of use. Miss a day, it resets. Simple accountability that keeps you showing up.

🔊

Ambient Audio

Built-in Brown Noise, Rain, and 432Hz tone. Synthesized on-device — no extra apps or browser tabs needed.

⌨️

Keyboard Control

J/K to navigate, Space to complete, E to edit. Full command palette with Ctrl+K. No mouse required.

💾

Backup & Restore

Export all tasks as a .json file in one click. Import them back on any machine — instant recovery.

🔒

Private by Design

No telemetry, no accounts, no cloud. Your data is stored locally in your Windows user profile and nowhere else.
